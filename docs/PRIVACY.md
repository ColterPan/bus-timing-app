# Privacy Policy: SG Bus Timing

_Effective 4 November 2026_

SG Bus Timing ("the app") shows Singapore bus arrival times. It is an independent app and is not affiliated with the Land Transport Authority (LTA).

## Summary

The app does **not** collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

## Location

- The app asks for your location **only** to show bus stops near you and your position on the map.
- Your location is used **on your device only**. It is never sent to our server or to anyone else, and it is not stored.
- You can say no, or turn location off at any time in your phone's settings. Search, favourites and the MRT map still work without it.

## Data stored on your phone

These stay on your device and are deleted if you uninstall the app or clear its data:

- your favourite bus stops and pinned bus services
- your theme choice (System / Light / Dark)
- a copy of Singapore's public bus stop list, so the app works faster

## Network requests

- **Bus arrival times:** the app sends a **bus stop code** (for example `83139`) to our server, which fetches the times from LTA DataMall. No location or personal information is sent. Like any web server, our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may briefly process your device's IP address to deliver the response and protect against abuse; we do not store or use it.
- **Map tiles** are loaded from OneMap (Singapore Land Authority), and the **MRT system map** image is loaded from LTA's website. These services receive standard web requests (such as your IP address) under their own privacy policies.

## Children

The app is a general-audience transport utility and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
